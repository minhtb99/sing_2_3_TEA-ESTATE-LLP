Black Tea – Our Ceylon Tea

Tea cultivation in Sri Lanka began in the 1800s during the British colonial era when it was known as Ceylon. The name “Ceylon tea” was retained to reflect the heritage and quality of Sri Lanka’s teas.

Ceylon black tea powder is made by drying and fermenting the leaves of the Camellia Sinensis plant.

Ceylon tea is medium-bodied with sweet, slightly fruity and floral notes. It can be enjoyed freshly brewed on its own, hot or cold. It is also versatile and can be used to make sweetened teas, milk teas and infusions with herbs, spices or fruits.

The Ceylon tea that we carry is of Orange Pekoe grade, 100% hand-picked, and is wholly processed in Sri Lanka.

Suitable as a treat for you and your loved ones, or as gifts for family and friends!
